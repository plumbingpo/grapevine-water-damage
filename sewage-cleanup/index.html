A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Grapevine, Grapevine Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Grapevine

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
