When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Grapevine

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
