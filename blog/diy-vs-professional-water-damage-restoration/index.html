When to Hire a Water Damage Professional in Grapevine, TX

Home / Blog / DIY vs Professional Water Damage Restoration in Grapevine, TX

After a leak or flood at a house in Grapevine, TX, you have to decide quickly between doing the cleanup yourself and hiring professionals. The instinct to save money by doing it yourself is understandable, but most water damage is far more complex than it appears on the surface. Read on to learn where the line sits between a do-it-yourself job and one that calls for professional restoration.

When Doing It Yourself Can Work

Very small, contained water events may be manageable without professional help. Clean water, such as a sink overflow, that touched only non-porous hard floors, stayed below roughly 10 square feet, and was removed within the hour is usually fine to deal with yourself. The same goes for a little water on vinyl or tile: mop it right away, run fans and a dehumidifier, and a professional is probably not needed. Four things have to be true: clean water, a small area, immediate cleanup, and porous materials that were not significantly affected.

Situations That Need a Professional

In practice, the majority of water losses need a professional restoration crew. Do not wait to call if any of these apply: a musty odor that points to early mold, a source you are unable to locate or stop, possible water in wall cavities or framing, contaminated water (black or gray), wet drywall, carpet, or similar porous materials, several hours of standing water, or a wet area bigger than 10 square feet.

What DIY Cleanup Can Really Cost

In Grapevine, a do-it-yourself cleanup often costs the homeowner more in the end than hiring a restoration company would have. Here is why: incomplete drying leads to mold growth within days, adding $1,500 to $6,000 or more in remediation costs. Wet materials that are dried incorrectly keep breaking down, so more has to be replaced. The cost of rented extraction and drying gear, fans and dehumidifiers included, mounts quickly while performing below a professional setup. If the cleanup is poorly documented, your insurance payout may be smaller. Meanwhile the damage keeps compounding, since proper restoration cannot start until the unsuccessful DIY effort ends.

The Professional Advantage

Professional water damage restoration provides several advantages that DIY cannot match: truck-mounted extraction that removes water hundreds of times faster, commercial drying equipment that actually dries wall cavities and structural materials, thermal imaging that finds hidden moisture, monitoring that verifies complete drying, antimicrobial treatment that helps prevent mold, thorough documentation that supports your insurance claim, and the peace of mind of knowing the job is done right.
